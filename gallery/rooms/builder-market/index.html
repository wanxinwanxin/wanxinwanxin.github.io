What you see. One ridge per week since the Merge, printed from top to bottom. The ridge height is the share of blocks that each builder made, in a fixed left-to-right order. The ticker scrolls through every slot of the last seven days, one cell per block, colored by builder. The HHI panel shows how concentrated the market is (10000 means one builder makes every block).

What you hear. An arpeggio with one pentatonic note per builder. The notes play in proportion to each builder's share in the current week, so a concentrated market sounds like one repeated note and a diverse market sounds like a run. The filter opens when the market is diverse. The low pulse is the ticker: one pulse per block, with a pitch that follows the base fee.

Source: BigQuery public data set bigquery-public-data.crypto_ethereum.blocks (Google Cloud public data sets, blockchain-etl), builder labels from the block extra_data tag. README with the full tag mapping and the limits of the method.
