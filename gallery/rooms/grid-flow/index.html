THE GRID BREATHES

EIA-930 HOURLY INTERCHANGE // US BALANCING AUTHORITIES // 2024 LOADING
WHAT YOU SEE. Each dot is a balancing authority, sized by its demand. Green dots export power, amber dots import it. Particles run along the tie lines in the direction of the flow. More particles mean more megawatts. WHAT YOU HEAR. The hum rises with total demand and brightens when solar rises. The breath of noise is wind power. Each ping is a tie line above 2,500 MW, panned east or west by its longitude.
