The Citation Galaxy

Supreme Court opinions · 1754 – 2015

Stars are opinions, old at the center. Lines are citations. Plucks are citations to bright stars.

What you see and hear

Every star is a Supreme Court opinion. Old opinions sit near the center and new opinions near the rim. A line is a citation. It flashes in the year of the citing opinion and then settles. Gold stars are the 300 most cited opinions. Constellations join the brightest stars of one citation community.

The drone moves its root with each century. Each citation to a bright star plucks a string. Deep notes mark famous cases. The pan follows the star's position.

Source: Caselaw Access Project, United States Reports volumes 1 – 572 (static.case.law), hosted by the Free Law Project. Read the README
