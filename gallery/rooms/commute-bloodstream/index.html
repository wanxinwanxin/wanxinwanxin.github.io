The Commute Bloodstream

Chicago, one working day. Every particle is a group of workers that travel from the tract where they live to the tract where they work, and back. The city has a pulse: two beats, morning and evening.

1 particle = 20 workers
What you see. Each point of light is a group of workers on the way between home and work. The curve under the timeline is the pulse: how many workers move at each minute. What you hear. A crowd of soft noise grains. The grain rate follows the number of workers in motion. Each grain pans left or right with the longitude of the particle. A wooden tick marks each 15 minutes. A low tone swells at the two rush-hour peaks.

Source: US Census Bureau, LEHD LODES8 origin-destination file for Illinois (2022, all jobs). Tract centroids: Census Gazetteer 2023. Time of departure: ACS 2022 5-year, table B08302. LODES has no time of day. The departure times here come from the ACS distribution of each home tract. The return trip starts 9 hours after departure.
