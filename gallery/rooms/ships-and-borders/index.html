Ships at the Border

Channel Islands National Marine Sanctuary · AIS vessel tracks, 15 to 18 June 2023

—

What you see

Four days of ship positions around the Channel Islands. Each vessel is a small ink mark that moves along its track. The ink darkens where ships repeat a path. Hatched areas are state and federal marine reserves. The dashed blue line is the sanctuary boundary. The dotted sepia bands are the charted traffic lanes. The thin lines around the land are decorative, in the manner of a printed chart.

What you hear

A sonar ping sounds when a vessel enters a hatched reserve. The pitch tells the vessel class. A softer, lower ping marks an entry into the sanctuary. A foghorn sounds when a ship 200 m or longer enters the traffic lanes. Each sound comes from the side of the chart where the ship is. The wave noise rises with the number of vessels under way.
