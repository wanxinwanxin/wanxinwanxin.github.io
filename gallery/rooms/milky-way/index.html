A Million Neighbors

Every point is a real star within about 100 parsecs of the Sun, at its measured position, in its measured color, from ESA Gaia. The camera drifts around the Sun. In time mode the stars move along their measured motions, thousands of years per second. The drone deepens as more stars come into view. Each sparkle is a star that passes close to the camera: blue stars ring high, red stars ring low, bright stars ring louder.

331,000 real stars within 100 parsecs of the Sun, from ESA Gaia, in their measured colors and motions. The drone deepens with the number of stars in view. Each sparkle is a star that passes close to the camera: blue high, red low, bright loud.
