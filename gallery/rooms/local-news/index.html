      What you see

      Each state is a halftone. The dots grow with the share of newspaper pages in that state and decade whose OCR text contains the chosen word. The dial steps through 17 decades. In the headline mode, each state types the word that stands out most against its all-time national share.

      You hear a typewriter key for each letter, panned to where the state sits. A press rumble gets louder and brighter as the national share rises. A page turns at every decade.
