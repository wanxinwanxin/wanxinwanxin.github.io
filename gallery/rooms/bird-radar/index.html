What you see. The sweep paints one radar scan per turn: one hour before sunset, one hour after, then 2.5 hours after. On a clear spring night the scope fills with a ring of green echo. That ring is birds, aloft by the million. Amber marks rain-like echo. The pen recorder below tracks the biological index across all 36 nights.

What you hear. Chirps are the migration. Their rate follows the biological index of the scan on the scope, and they pan toward the bearing of the strongest biological echo. A soft whoosh marks each sweep. A rain hiss rises with the share of rain-like echo. A night chorus of filtered noise sits underneath.
