Direction

A ribbon starts at the source arc and ends with an arrow at the destination arc. For a pair bridge, such as the Arbitrum canonical bridge, the data gives both ends. Ethereum deposits go to the rollup. Withdrawals come back to Ethereum.

The hub

Multi-chain bridges (Across, Relay, CCTP, LayerZero, Wormhole and others) report value per chain and not per route. Value that leaves a chain through them flows into the hub. Value that arrives flows out of the hub. The hub arc therefore counts each transfer twice, once on each end.

Sound

Each network has a fixed note on a pentatonic scale, panned to its position on the ring. A bell sounds for a network whose monthly volume grew. The soft pad gets louder with the total monthly volume.
